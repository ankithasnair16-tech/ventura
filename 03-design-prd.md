# Ventura: Design PRD

## 1. Design goal
Make Ventura look polished and easy to scan on a phone. It should feel warm and natural, and the sustainable alternatives section should stand out.

## 2. Style direction
These are general style qualities only. Do not copy any specific layout, logo, image, or brand from any reference.
- **Overall feel:** a modern mobile app look, with rounded cards, a big bold title, and soft shadows.
- **Type and writing:** bold, all-caps headlines with clean sans-serif body text, short confident sentences, and bold labels or badges.

## 3. Colors (earthy palette)
- Background: warm sand or off-white.
- Main text: deep brown or charcoal.
- Primary accent: olive or forest green.
- Secondary accent: clay or terracotta.
- Cards: soft cream with subtle shadows.
- Air-quality status colors are used only for the status label, so the rest of the palette stays earthy. Use green for good, yellow for moderate, orange for poor, red for very poor, and purple for hazardous.
- Every color comes with a text label, so color is never the only signal.

## 4. Typography
- **Headlines:** bold, all caps. Example: "BREATHE BETTER. LIVE CLEANER."
- **Body:** a clean sans-serif, such as Inter or a similar free font. Size at least 16 px on mobile.
- **Labels and badges:** bold, short.
- Keep sentences short. Avoid long paragraphs on screen.

## 5. Home screen layout (top to bottom)
1. **Header:** the name "Ventura" with a small leaf icon.
2. **Headline:** a big bold line, for example "BREATHE BETTER. LIVE CLEANER."
3. **Air status card:** Delhi's current level as a large number, a color, and a short label such as "Very Poor." The main pollutant appears below it.
4. **Quick start card:** the 3 to 4 questions, shown as large tappable options.
5. **Four area cards:**
   - Daily routines.
   - Indoor air hacks.
   - Sustainable alternatives (the largest card, with a highlighted border or badge).
   - Impact of actions.
6. **Chat button:** a round button in the bottom corner, labeled "Ask Ventura."

## 6. Card style
- Rounded corners, about 16 px radius.
- Soft shadow, so cards look layered.
- Generous space between cards.
- Each card has one icon, one title, and one short line.

## 7. Sustainable alternatives section
- Shown as a highlighted card near the top of the results.
- Shows two or three options at a time, each with a "Not for me" button.
- Each option shows its impact level and a one-line benefit.

## 8. Chat screen
- Opens as a full-screen panel on mobile, or a side panel on a laptop.
- A clear "Ask about sustainable alternatives" prompt at the top.
- Replies appear as short cards, not long text.
- A visible counter or note showing the chat is limited to a few messages.
- A "Clear chat" button.

## 9. Mobile and desktop
- Design for a phone first, with one column of cards.
- On a laptop, show the cards in two columns.
- Touch targets at least 44 px.

## 10. Accessibility
- Text contrast at least 4.5 to 1.
- Labels on all buttons and fields.
- Clear focus outlines for keyboard users.
- Works with screen magnifiers and larger browser text.

## 11. Motion
- Keep animations minimal: one soft fade-in for the cards. No autoplay.

## 12. Visual quality checklist
- Consistent spacing and corner radius throughout.
- One font family for the body, one for headlines.
- No more than four main colors on the screen at once, plus status colors.
- Every card has the same structure.
